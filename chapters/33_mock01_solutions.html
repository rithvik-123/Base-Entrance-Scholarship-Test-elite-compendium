Q1. Answer: 072

Step 1 (Divisibility by $36$): Since $\gcd(4, 9) = 1$, the 6-digit number $47A25B$ must be simultaneously divisible by $4$ and by $9$.

Step 2 (Apply Rule of $4$ first): The last two digits $5B$ must be divisible by $4$. With $B > 0$, the only valid digits are $B = 2$ ($52 = 4 \times 13$) and $B = 6$ ($56 = 4 \times 14$).

Step 3 (Apply Rule of $9$): Sum of digits $= 4 + 7 + A + 2 + 5 + B = 18 + A + B$. Because $18$ is already a multiple of $9$, we require $A + B \equiv 0 \pmod{9}$, so $A + B \in \{9, 18\}$ (impossible for $A+B=0$ since $B>0$).

  • If $B = 2 \implies A = 7$ (valid digit) $\implies 10A + B = 10(7) + 2 = 72$.
  • If $B = 6 \implies A = 3$ (valid digit) $\implies 10A + B = 10(3) + 6 = 36$.

Maximum Value: $72 \implies$ bubble 072.

30-Second Speed Trick: Maximize the tens digit $A$ first! Since $10A + B$ is dominated by $A$, immediately test the smallest allowed $B$ ($B = 2$), which forces the largest possible $A = 9 - 2 = 7$, locking in $72$ in 12 seconds.
