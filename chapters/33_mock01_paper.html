Q1. A 6-digit number $47A25B$ is completely divisible by $36$. If $B > 0$, find the maximum possible value of $10A + B$.

Q2. Let $u$ be the unit digit of the product $37^{2026} \times 43^{2027}$, and let $v$ be the remainder when $2^{50}$ is divided by $7$. Find the value of $10u + v$.

Q3. A vessel contains $80\text{ L}$ of pure milk. First, $20\text{ L}$ of milk is drawn out and replaced with water. Next, $16\text{ L}$ of the resulting mixture is drawn out and replaced with water. Find the final quantity of pure milk (in litres) left in the vessel.

Q4. A motorboat travels $45\text{ km}$ downstream and $27\text{ km}$ upstream in a total time of $6\text{ hours}$. The same boat travels $30\text{ km}$ downstream and $36\text{ km}$ upstream in a total time of $6\text{ hours}$. Find the speed of the boat in still water (in $\text{km/h}$).

Q5. Two alloys $P$ and $Q$ contain copper and zinc in the mass ratios $5:3$ and $7:5$, respectively. If $16\text{ kg}$ of alloy $P$ is melted together with $24\text{ kg}$ of alloy $Q$, find the total mass of copper (in $\text{kg}$) in the resulting alloy.

Q6. Tap $A$ can fill an empty cistern in $12\text{ hours}$, and Tap $B$ can fill it in $18\text{ hours}$. A drain pipe $C$ at the bottom can empty the full cistern in $36\text{ hours}$. All three pipes are opened together for $4\text{ hours}$, after which Tap $A$ is closed. How many additional hours will Tap $B$ and drain pipe $C$ together take to fill the remaining part of the cistern?

Q7. A trader marks his goods $40\%$ above cost price and offers a $15\%$ discount on the marked price. In addition, due to a faulty balance, he delivers only $850\text{ g}$ while charging for $1\text{ kg}$. Find his net profit percentage.

Q8. Find the total number of trailing zeros at the end of the product: $$N = 15^{12} \times 24^{10} \times 35^{15} \times 40^{8}$$

Q9. A swimmer swims $24\text{ km}$ upstream from point $P$ to point $Q$ against a river current of $2\text{ km/h}$ and immediately returns downstream from $Q$ to $P$. If his average speed for the entire round trip is $9.6\text{ km/h}$, find the swimmer's speed in still water (in $\text{km/h}$).

Q10. When a positive integer $N$ is divided by $7$, it leaves a remainder of $4$. When the same integer $N$ is divided by $11$, it leaves a remainder of $6$. Find the largest 3-digit value of $N$.
