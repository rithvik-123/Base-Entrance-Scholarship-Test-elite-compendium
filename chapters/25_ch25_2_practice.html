Passage I (Questions 16 & 17) — Industrial Extraction of Aluminium

Red bauxite ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3\cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$), the chief ore of aluminium, is heavily contaminated with basic iron(III) oxide ($\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$) and silica ($\text{SiO}_2$). Before electrolytic reduction, bauxite is purified via Bayer's Process: powdered ore is digested with hot concentrated $\text{NaOH}(aq)$, leaving $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$ behind as insoluble red mud while aluminium dissolves as soluble compound $X$. Passing $\text{CO}_2$ through the clear filtrate precipitates pure $\text{Al(OH)}_3$, which is calcined to anhydrous alumina ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$, $\text{MP} \approx 2050^\circ\text{C}$). In the Hall–Héroult cell, pure alumina is dissolved in molten cryolite ($\text{Na}_3\text{AlF}_6$) and fluorspar ($\text{CaF}_2$) and electrolyzed at $\approx 950^\circ\text{C}$ using a carbon-lined steel vessel as cathode and suspended graphite rods as anodes.

Passage II (Questions 24 & 25) — Auto-Reduction & Refining of Copper Glance

Copper is extracted from its concentrated sulfide ore, Copper Glance ($\text{Cu}_2\text{S}$), without adding any external carbon reducing agent. First, a controlled two-thirds fraction of $\text{Cu}_2\text{S}$ is roasted in air to cuprous oxide ($\text{Cu}_2\text{O}$). The air supply is then cut off and the furnace temperature is raised, allowing the remaining unroasted $\text{Cu}_2\text{S}$ to undergo auto-reduction with $\text{Cu}_2\text{O}$ ($2\text{Cu}_2\text{O} + \text{Cu}_2\text{S} \xrightarrow{\Delta} 6\text{Cu} + \text{SO}_2$) to yield blister copper ($\approx 98\%$ pure). Finally, blister copper is cast into thick anodes and electrolytically refined in acidified $\text{CuSO}_4(aq)$ to obtain $99.99\%$ pure electrical-grade copper.
