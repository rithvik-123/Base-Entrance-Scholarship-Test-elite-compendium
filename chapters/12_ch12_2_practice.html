Rules of Engagement

NAT (Numerical Answer Type): the answer is an integer from 000 to 999. Write it as three digits (a result of $7$ is entered as $007$).

MCQ: exactly one option is correct. Passage sets: two MCQs share one passage and one figure.

Time budget: Level 1 ≈ 2 min each · Level 2 ≈ 4–5 min each · Level 3 ≈ 8 min each. A figure is drawn only where a question refers to it; diagrams are schematic unless a length is printed on them. Units are cm unless stated.
