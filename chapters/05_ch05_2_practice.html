How to Use This Arena
  • Pacing target: about 2 minutes per Level 1 question, 4 per Level 2 and 6 per Level 3, roughly 2 hours in total.
  • NAT answers are integers from 000 to 999. Write them with three digits and leading zeros (for example $7\to007$).
  • Quantitative Comparison options are always: (A) Column A is greater, (B) Column B is greater, (C) the two columns are equal, (D) the relation cannot be determined.
  • Clockwise and "left/right" are always as seen from above and from the person's own viewpoint, as taught in Part 1. Do not look at the answer key until you have committed to an answer for every question.
